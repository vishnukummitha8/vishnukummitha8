DIGITAL WORK INSTRUCTION

Strut Assembly Steering Knuckle Stabilizer Bar Lower Control Arm

Step 1 : Install Lower Control Arm

  1. Scan Lower Control Arm (LH and RH).
  2. Position lower control arm on the subframe.
  3. Tighten front and rear bolts as per sequence.
  4. Ensure all bolts are tightened to target torque.

Step 2 : Install Strut Assembly

  1. Scan Strut Assembly (LH and RH).
  2. Align strut top mount with body shell holes.
  3. Hand-start all three top nuts before torquing.
  4. Torque top nuts to 65 Nm in a criss-cross pattern.

Step 3 : Install Steering Knuckle

  1. Scan Steering Knuckle (LH and RH).
  2. Seat knuckle into strut bottom fork.
  3. Insert bottom bolts and torque to 120 Nm.
  4. Connect ball joint and secure the cotter pin.

Step 4 : Install Stabilizer Bar & Links

  1. Scan Stabilizer Bar and both Stabilizer Links.
  2. Mount bar with bushes and clamp brackets.
  3. Fit links between bar ends and strut brackets.
  4. Torque link nuts to 45 Nm; verify free movement.

Step 5 : Final Torque & Inspection

  1. Verify all 8 torque points show OK status.
  2. Apply torque paint mark on each fastener.
  3. Perform visual check for clearance and routing.
  4. Confirm quality checklist and complete station.
SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS
  • Ensure vehicle is in safe position.
  • Do not place hands under suspension.
  • Wear safety gloves and shoes.
  • Follow ergonomic lifting procedure.
