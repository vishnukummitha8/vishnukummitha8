Digital Work Instruction

Step 1: Install Lower Control Arm

  1. Position LH Lower Control Arm onto subframe mount points.
  2. Insert pivot bolts loosely — do not fully torque yet.
  3. Align ball joint with steering knuckle taper.
  4. Verify LH/RH orientation mark faces outward.
  5. Scan part barcode to confirm genealogy capture.
SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS
  • Wear cut-resistant gloves and safety glasses.
  • Ensure lift assist is engaged before handling strut.
  • Do not place hands under suspended components.
  • Verify torque tool calibration before use.
